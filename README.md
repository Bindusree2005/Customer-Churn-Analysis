# Telecom Customer Churn & Revenue Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer churn patterns and monthly charges in the telecommunications industry using Power BI.

The objective is to understand customer behavior, identify customer segments associated with higher churn, analyze monthly charges at risk, and generate actionable business insights to support customer retention strategies.

## Tools & Technologies

- Power BI Desktop
- Power Query
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)

## Project Objectives

- Analyze overall customer churn and retention.
- Identify churn patterns across different contract types.
- Analyze churn based on internet services and payment methods.
- Understand the relationship between customer tenure and churn.
- Identify monthly charges associated with churned customers.
- Generate actionable recommendations to improve customer retention.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes the following KPIs:

1. Total Customers
2. Churned Customers
3. Churn Rate (%)
4. Monthly Revenue (Total Monthly Charges)
5. Average Monthly Charges
6. Retained Customers
7. Average Tenure (Months)
8. Monthly Charges at Risk

## Dashboard Pages

### Page 1 – Customer Churn Overview

Provides an overview of customer churn using KPI cards and interactive visualizations.

Visualizations include:
- Customer Churn by Contract Type
- Customer Churn by Internet Service
- Churn Rate by Tenure Group
- Customer Churn by Payment Method

### Page 2 – Revenue & Customer Insights

Focuses on customer distribution and monthly charges associated with churned customers.

Visualizations include:
- Monthly Charges at Risk by Contract Type
- Customer Distribution by Tenure Group
- Churned Customers by Senior Citizen Status
- Average Monthly Charges by Churn Status

The page also presents key business insights and recommendations.

## Key Business Insights

- Month-to-month contracts have the highest number of churned customers.
- Fiber optic customers account for the highest number of churned customers.
- Customers with 0–12 months of tenure have the highest churn rate.
- Electronic check users represent the largest group of churned customers.
- Month-to-month contracts have the highest monthly charges at risk.

## Business Recommendations

- Improve onboarding and engagement for new customers.
- Investigate customer dissatisfaction among fiber optic users.
- Encourage month-to-month customers to switch to longer-term contracts.
- Review the payment experience for electronic check users.
- Develop targeted customer retention strategies.

## Interactive Features

- Interactive KPI cards and charts.
- Contract, Internet Service, and Payment Method slicers.
- Synchronized slicers across both dashboard pages.
- Dynamic filtering and cross-interaction between visuals.
